For `W` eligible weeks, the necessary count is `21 * W`, not signals, partial reductions, unfunded intents or separately counted families. A possibility claim is NOT economic evidence.
- **Potential future conflict:** `abstain` on no-edge weeks versus mandatory HARD21 is a constraint conflict *if* a qualifying eligible week lacks 21 safe profitable feasible completions; it has not been proven universal. Report dated instrument/path/volume/margin and qualified-round-trip evidence before declaring infeasibility.
