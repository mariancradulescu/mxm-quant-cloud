## What was observed and what was estimated

166 complete historical development cost contexts were recovered out of180 planned
symbol/windows. Every sampled spread comes from genuine historical BID/ASK with
route-local quote/FX age checks. There are6–18 samples and3–6 dates per instrument.
Current commission/margin/volume/funding terms remain current-term scenarios;
no historical tariff continuity is asserted. Observed spread means are roughly
0.029–1.578 bps across assets, distinct from full strategy round-trip costs.

Time/volatility conditioning is used only when sample/date support permits.
The7,221 adverse selection proposals use1,490 instrument/hour/volatility estimates,
3,337 hour fallbacks and2,394 instrument-global fallbacks. These overlapping proposal
counts are not effective cost-sample counts. Sparse calibration dates do not
establish annual liquidity-tail coverage. Even an illustrative IID-date calculation
puts the probability that the observed date maximum covers a95th percentile at
only14.3–26.5%; IID dates themselves are not established. The adverse uncertainty
adjustment and stress case are declared scenarios, not certified upper bounds.

Slippage is NOT historically measured. It is modeled per side from declared
spread multiples plus a diffusion-scaled1s volatility proxy. Funding ignores
credits and uses three current directional daily debits at each current-schedule
crossing; the percentage-swap conversion uses a365-day annual divisor assumption.
The broker's historical day basis/rates/schedules are not certified. Conversion
uses the observed/current-term adverse credit/debit wedge. BID-return proxies
pay one modeled spread, then separate commission/slippage/funding/conversion;
spread is not charged twice. No synthetic ASK is presented as an authentic fill.
Nominal bar-end information clocks do not certify actual historical1s execution.
