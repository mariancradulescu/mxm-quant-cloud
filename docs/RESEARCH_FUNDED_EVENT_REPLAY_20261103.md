# Quote-event EUR200 funded replay — research-only boundary (2026-11-03)

## Why this exists
Previous exact candidates and their historical receipts remain frozen. This *new research-only calculation primitive* is necessary to test future **distinct economic hypotheses** without treating bar OHLC as broker fills or evaluating isolated trades as if each had a fresh EUR200 bankroll. It does not alter or extend the final C# cBot and creates no separate repository, server, credential, workflow or order capability. Source: `research/funded_replay.py`; synthetic invariants: `research/test_funded_replay.py`.

### Required supplied evidence before any authentic run
A causal strategy supplies a complete timestamped record of what it knew and when (`signal_available_at`, `calibrated_through`) and an `OPEN` / `CLOSE` decision timestamp. Quotes must be actual instrument **BID/ASK** and independently reconstructed contemporaneous quote-currency-to-EUR debit/credit rates. The caller must bind `source_hash` to cryptographically reconciled Pepperstone source; this module **does not independently authenticate the input string**. Contract data must be in actual base units, EUR per-unit margin and observed commission terms; all quantities, tariff versions and conversion paths must be validated in the protected pipeline. Quoted 1,000-unit minimums and cent-based Open API broker fields must be correctly decoded by the caller, never guessed here. A hypothetical historical tariff cannot be called authentic.

An entry is filled at the **first subsequent observed quote** after explicit positive model latency, never at the signal bar's OHLC. Long entries cross ASK and exits BID; shorts enter BID and exit ASK. Slippage is an explicit hypothetical per-side unfavorable price displacement and never a measured real fill. Stops/targets become delayed **software exit requests** on executable-side quotes; a price gap can pass the stop, and the next quote determines the modeled exit. There are no guaranteed native stop fills or real orders.

Each new position competes with all currently open positions for the **single initial EUR200**, marked-to-liquidation equity and aggregate margin. No universal 1% / 2% sizing policy is imposed. This is a funding-feasibility replay, **not** the predictive model or adaptive risk selector: candidates must be sized and compared with justified joint loss scenarios by upstream research. Borrowing, portfolio netting offsets and future price knowledge are never assumed. A stale quote needed for simultaneous equity marking makes the evidence incomplete rather than assuming the prior favorable price.

Accounting uses Decimal and decomposes mid-reference gross EUR, bid/ask spread, per-side slippage proxy, conservative signed FX conversion cost, commissions and explicit signed financing credits/debits. Full-week HARD21 counting is provided for *caller-supplied ex-ante eligible UTC Mondays*, including zero-trade weeks. No automatic retroactive week eligibility. Only complete round trips count. Currency conversion paths and financing schedules must be independently source-proven upstream; a boolean supplied to the kernel does not establish that proof.

### Explicit non-claims / unimplemented operational fidelity
- Existing genuine Pepperstone archives have **not yet** been connected to this generic event engine. Its regression tests are **synthetic only**; therefore it produces **zero new authentic trade episodes, no NET edge and no independent confirmation**.
- Equity marks and drawdown are measured only at observed quote timestamps, never certified global intratick extrema; missing feed intervals, future gaps, unrepresented slippage and stopout timing remain unresolved.
- Static caller-supplied margin per unit does **not** model all future dynamic leverage tiers, fee schedule changes, rollovers or actual order acceptance. The modeled fill size, delay and spread do not prove executed liquidity or live broker stopout rules. Margin and cost evidence at decision time must be supplied per candidate.
- The module does not learn regimes, recalibrate models, handle partial fills, model uncertain submissions, or infer broker order state. No automatic trading is possible.
- `economic_qualification`, `independent_confirmation`, `real_fills` and `global_drawdown_certified` deliberately remain false in every report. A flat ledger reconciles balance to EUR200 plus complete net trades, but never upgrades itself into a certified profit claim.
