## 1. OWNER objectives and mandatory constraints

- Deliver one autonomous native C# cBot for cTrader Cloud, manageable on Android, in this repository. Use the same authoritative C# causal features, model, EUR allocator, fill and cost handling, position lifecycle and accounting in Core, Replay and the cBot adapter.
- One common EUR300 initial book per configuration. Keep actual and simulated funding domains distinct. Maintain cash, margin, realized and floating equity through the entire evaluation, with no deposits or resets. Retained gains stay in the same portfolio. Maintain concurrent Held + Pending commitments and size only in supported contract increments.
- Use the broadest relevant official multi-asset, multi-timeframe and multi-family evidence. Economic decisions use closed bars and information available at the decision. Evaluate LONG, SHORT, WAIT, HOLD, REDUCE and CLOSE jointly with risk, sizing and opportunity cost.
- Require recurrent positive BRUT in EUR and robust NET after costs, including adverse cost scenarios. HARD21 means at least 21 legally financed and fully closed round trips **in each prospectively eligible system week**. Aggregate the one portfolio. Partial reductions and signals do not count. Preserve the right to abstain.
- Maximum portfolio drawdown is 30% including floating equity and costs. Disclose unavailable intervals and risk beyond intended stops. Size and reserve for execution uncertainty and aggregate exposure. Exclude intentional cash resets, martingale and unsupported risk escalation.
- Require at least twelve relevant months of genuinely independent confirmation before economic promotion. Authenticate the source, show the exposure and chronology, and demonstrate common-book evidence over that period. An existing historical interval can qualify if these conditions are proven.
- Use only authorized official cTrader/Spotware sources and capabilities. The existing Pepperstone LIVE account is authorized for reading through the original identity binding, operation allowlist and protected custody. Token scope can exceed that authority. Preserve raw packets and credentials in the protected environment. During research the allowed order and Cloud-activation counts are both zero. OWNER alone authorizes any later activation.
- The product initializes, discovers its official universe, accesses recoverable history, reconstructs context, calibrates causally, and recovers account state on its own. Certify actual Cloud/Android operation and recovery separately from build parity. The target requires no permanent external service, manual GitHub operation or desktop dependency.
