## Active methodological policy v1 (prospectively revisable)

Evidence levels: (1) exploratory economic hypothesis; (2) preliminary predictive
and economic support; (3) demonstrated possible incremental portfolio contribution;
(4) statistically, financially and operationally qualified portfolio; (5) native
product ready for separate authorization. Individual candidates need not meet
system HARD21. Level assertions alone confer no qualification or trading rights.

Use one causal opportunity representation and native allocator, the existing
funded event replay during development, and a shared capital ledger. At a decision
clock only previously observable data and matured calibration labels may rank or
size opportunities. Joint scenarios use identical economic states and account for
existing/selected exposure, uncertainty, costs, margin, drawdown and favorable
peaks. Never assume unprofitable components become profitable in combination.
Evaluate marginal contributions and interaction effects on independent data.

Maintain `research/evidence_registry.json` as a conservative global source/exposure
inventory, not a claim that untouched confirmation exists. Calendar overlap across
studies/instruments can affect design; model-local nonuse is insufficient. Unknown
exposure means not independent. Register trial families/universe, policy and splits
before scoring; account for multiplicity and shared data. No universal 60/20/20
ratio. Freeze each existing study's own allocation. Future studies justify their
own separation, embargo and sample size. Do not manufacture annual performance.

## Temporary implementation decisions and present limits

`OpportunityOrchestrator` v1 greedily chooses positive lower marginal log utility,
using the existing `AdaptivePortfolioRisk`, and reranks after reserving each
allocation. Explicit joint state IDs prevent accidental scenario independence;
redundancy keys and conflicting symbol directions are conservatively excluded.
This is a research heuristic, not a globally optimal or economically validated
orchestrator. Conservative loss summation supplies no diversification credit.
Existing exposure management/forced liquidation is not implemented by this kernel.

`JointOpportunityResearch` JOP1 is a development probe on all available original
six-asset H4 source: fixed six-bar drift and last-bar reversal descriptors, first
80% only, no fitting/selection or NET claims. It neither imports nor promotes a
closed model. Current source lacks matching executable H4 quotes and joint EUR
risk history; the native selector therefore must decline all funded entries.
This measures coverage and conflicts, not profitable allocation. No C# or Python
simulator capability may be reported as confirmed economic orchestration.

The Python adapter invokes the native selector and feeds admitted decisions into
`FundedReplay`; it does not implement a second allocator. Full authentic allocation
parity, synchronized marks/costs, portfolio ablations on independent evidence,
continuous drawdown/restart proof, and Android/Cloud acceptance remain outstanding.
