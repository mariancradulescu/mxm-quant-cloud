## 1. OWNER objectives and mandatory constraints

- Deliver one autonomous native C# cBot for cTrader Cloud, manageable on Android, in this repository. Use the same authoritative C# causal features, model, EUR allocator, fill and cost handling, position lifecycle and accounting in Core, Replay and the cBot adapter.
- One common EUR300 initial book per configuration. Keep actual and simulated funding domains distinct. Maintain cash, margin, realized and floating equity through the entire evaluation, with no deposits or resets. Retained gains stay in the same portfolio. Maintain concurrent Held + Pending commitments and size only in supported contract increments.
- Use the broadest relevant official multi-asset, multi-timeframe and multi-family evidence. Economic decisions use closed bars and information available at the decision. Evaluate LONG, SHORT, WAIT, HOLD, REDUCE and CLOSE jointly with risk, sizing and opportunity cost.
- Require recurrent positive BRUT in EUR and robust NET after costs, including adverse cost scenarios. HARD21 means at least 21 legally financed and fully closed round trips **in each prospectively eligible system week**. Aggregate the one portfolio. Partial reductions and signals do not count. Preserve the right to abstain.
- Maximum portfolio drawdown is 30% including floating equity and costs. Disclose unavailable intervals and risk beyond intended stops. Size and reserve for execution uncertainty and aggregate exposure. Exclude intentional cash resets, martingale and unsupported risk escalation.
- Require at least twelve relevant months of genuinely independent confirmation before economic promotion. Authenticate the source, show the exposure and chronology, and demonstrate common-book evidence over that period. An existing historical interval can qualify if these conditions are proven.
- Use only authorized official cTrader/Spotware sources and capabilities. The existing Pepperstone LIVE account is authorized for reading through the original identity binding, operation allowlist and protected custody. Token scope can exceed that authority. Preserve raw packets and credentials in the protected environment. During research the allowed order and Cloud-activation counts are both zero. OWNER alone authorizes any later activation.
- The product initializes, discovers its official universe, accesses recoverable history, reconstructs context, calibrates causally, and recovers account state on its own. Certify actual Cloud/Android operation and recovery separately from build parity. The target requires no permanent external service, manual GitHub operation or desktop dependency.

## 3. Research and validation

Register the actual trial family, source identity, information availability, fitted quantities, benchmark, splits, financial assumptions, selection rule and outputs. A separate identity is sufficient to examine an improved configuration, reproduce a result or isolate a technical defect. Keep trial records and negative outcomes. Distinguish development, chronological tests inside development, and independent confirmation. Exposed data remain useful for development. Uncertain exposure stays unqualified for independent confirmation.

Use chronological fit and test, walk-forward updates where warranted, regime and instrument sensitivity, neighboring configurations, complexity control and trial-count/multiple-selection treatment appropriate to the question. Guard crossing labels and dependent samples using justified boundaries. Compare NET and capital with CASH and relevant funded controls on aligned clocks and contracts. Use genuinely independent evidence for promotion, even where the within-development comparison is later than its own fit. No universal minimum sample, fixed split, test statistic or significance threshold applies to every family. [Probability of backtest overfitting](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4w1110bb) · [Backtest overfitting in financial markets](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4hn4t174)

Inspect the global [exposure inventory](../research/evidence_registry.json), protocol windows, preserved sources and visibility of results before certifying an interval. Use recoverable authentic history immediately. If independent coverage is insufficient, document exact dates, symbols, absent data and exposure evidence while developing on eligible history. Collect the missing official facts that materially extend causal information, cost support or coverage. An exhaustive roster, completed annual dataset or prior profit is unnecessary for a focused collection or development pass.

Evaluate separate evidence of opportunity amplitude, predictive identification, selection, fully funded lifecycle and comparative EUR value. Historical terms projected from current observations and conservative cost estimates are conditional research. Track commission, spread, modeled slippage, conversion and financing separately. Retain unresolved facts without imputing zero or an authentic fill. Preserve continuous risk and genuinely independent economic evidence as separate acceptance requirements. Consider an additional interval of honest, causal protection observations where the decision grid leaves execution risk unresolved.

## 4. Optimizable hypotheses and parameters

OWNER authorizes legitimate reuse, calibration, recalibration, search over parameters and windows, refinements and alternatives of the same mechanism, both directions and controlled reproduction. Compare entry, exit, reward/risk, stop, target, size, protection, reduction, regime and allocator jointly. Reuse M1/M5/M15/M30/H1/H4/D1 as available and causally relevant. A missing optional feature stays missing. A mechanism that genuinely needs it can wait.

Use official Grid, genetic optimization and GetFitness where their environment and economics are supported. An equivalent search through the common C# ledger is appropriate when platform parity is outstanding. The ranking of experiments does not waive the portfolio constraints. Do not optimize to one maximum backtest or force HARD21. Time limits can be tested with a stated reason. Funding stays explicit through rollover. Risk reductions require executable evidence.

## 8. Execution and economic promotion

DIRECTOR executes authorized source work, implementation, optimization, reproducible evaluation and GitHub operations autonomously. AUDITOR independently verifies code, source exposure, custody, model selection, native/replay parity, financial integrity and results. OWNER sets the constraints, decides qualification and holds exclusive activation authority.

Promotion requires demonstrable recurrence and positive adverse NET on the common book, causal source and costs, systemic HARD21, floating drawdown within the OWNER limit, authentic independent confirmation and operational readiness for the intended level. Keep partial, conditional or unattested evidence at the supported level. Work can continue while a promotion requirement is outstanding.
