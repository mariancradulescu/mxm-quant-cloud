## Diagnosis: distinct failure mechanisms

1. **No incremental predictive information / comparator tautologies — direct proof, high confidence for exact families.** In HSD1, all 8 breakout and all 8 compression family/regime/horizon cells have exactly the same directional decision as its same-clock momentum comparator *by construction*; the original receipt reports zero incremental EUR in both TRAIN and SELECTION for these 16 cells. Across all 48 cells, 18 report numerical zero in both phases (two additional 3-bar-impulse cells happen to match observed comparator in the disclosed observations, not a proof of universal identity). HXP1 correct sign 50.19%, 44.90%, 45.45%; gross EUR negative in ALL phases, establishing that execution costs alone cannot explain that exact study's failure. **Likely impact:** no candidate-specific gross edge, so stronger filtering of economic costs cannot make a structural duplicate genuinely incremental. **Prospective remedy to test:** learn time-stamped conditional future distributions from historical *analog endpoints*, then compare full-state prediction against zero-drift, same-clock momentum and unconditional historical distribution; report direct full-minus-ablated incremental score. Never invert or refit HSD1/HXP1.

2. **Low effective statistical power, overlap and time nonstationarity — empirical symptoms certain, mechanism unproven.** HSD1 has 12/48 economic cells with under 60 training candidates, 4/48 with under 8 independent calendar weeks, 14/48 positive observed training commission-only sums but 0/48 with positive training weekly 3.30-SE lower bound vs cash; 15/48 change sign of commission-only sum between training and selection. Multi-horizon labels and same-market event clocks overlap; raw event counts significantly overstate independent opportunities. The 52-week HXC1 year is already *globally exposed* from previous work and cannot serve as independent confirmation. **Likely impact:** unstable estimated conditionals and chance-positive cells; no quantified causal attribution to regime shift yet. **Prospective remedy to test:** use predeclared rolling 13-week *matured-label* analog pool, per-endpoint effective neighbor support, explicit feature/model/evaluation warmup, calendar-week block statistics, and time-stable versus recency-only ablation. Compare training/selection weekly score drift and sample support. No dropping unfavorable months and no post-hoc regime boundary.

3. **Prediction-to-money execution gap — documented certainty for evidence class, net effect unknown.** HSD1 displays positive sign classification or observed positive commission-only values in some cells but zero economic admissions; for HXP1 even the optimistic GROSS source-price proxy is negative in three phases, so costs only deepen a negative mechanism there. CMA1 prices are 2026-11-04 *current original broker contract terms*, not timestamp-valid historical tariffs; HXC1 original OHLCV is not execution-grade historical BID/ASK at all decision clocks. Historical spread, swap, slippage, actual USD/EUR/JPY conversion and continuous funded C# equity are unobserved for these new experiments. **Likely impact:** full executable NET EUR cannot be proven; current commission-only EUR proxy may overstate historical profitability. **Prospective remedy to test:** separate a causal price-only signal layer from a disclosed current-tariff commission-only EUR sensitivity and admit to funded C# replay only if time-matched real quote/financing/contract data are independently available. Track cash, floating equity, conservative adverse costs and shared EUR300 margin before claiming HARD21 or DD compliance.

4. **Calendar coverage, warmup and incomplete forward paths — observed data limits, not proof of model bias.** HSD1 3,547 forward paths unknown; M1 exposes a much smaller complete-path subset (1,355/4,541) than M15 (13,534/13,664). Insufficient support can bias naïvely selected favorable signals if availability is conditioned on the outcome. **Likely impact:** right-censoring and uneven market/session representation, not inferable benefit from filling gaps. **Prospective remedy to test:** store source timestamp, observation/end timestamp, available-at, maturity timestamp and missing reason per endpoint. Every scheduled endpoint is counted; no forward fill; absent labels remain unknown and never condition generation of the prior prediction. Start per-instrument features only after 32 contiguous completed H1 bars and model after >=13 calendar weeks with mature labels. Re-assess complete/missing support separately by symbol/time segment.

## EWL1 design contract (to be frozen separately before any new protected evaluation)

At each completed historical H1 endpoint T, compute only causal OHLCV features as observed by T; obtain only matured analog outcomes with maturity <=T; use fixed 13-week rolling labeled history and recency weights, then make a full-state conditional predictive distribution with uncertainty and an explicit ABSTAIN. Run model ablations and baseline forecasts with identical endpoint eligibility and one fixed 4-hour price-proxy label. Evaluate every scheduled chronological endpoint, including missing features/labels, against naïve zero, unconditional own-symbol historical mean and 4-hour momentum. If price and matched benchmarks fail in chronological selection, DO NOT tune on the exposed tail or promote.

Three distinct warmups:
- **Feature:** 32 *consecutive completed* H1 candles, sufficient to build 24 returns, relative volatility and 4-hour impulse without using a missing-session gap.
- **Model:** 13 prior calendar weeks of eligible *matured-label* history, minimum 64 analog samples per instrument; a 13-week moving historical pool allows automatic expiration of stale examples without selectively deleting losers.
- **Evaluation:** 4-hour outcome maturity plus exact one-endpoint embargo; train/selection/exposed boundaries drop any crossing label. All statistics use calendar-week blocks, not naïve per-bar independent SE.

Hygiene: preserve immutable source encrypted artifacts, prove per-source raw proto and roster binding, exact UTC alignment, duplicate checks, chronological availability for symbol and cross-asset peers, missing windows/sessions, no date-filtering by realized return; recency weights and all parameters fixed before scoring. Never infer historical MarketDepth. The 12-asset H1 source is a distinct year-long backbone, while M15/M1 remain exposed support/forward-path evidence rather than pretending to be year-long causal features.
