# Opening dislocation after continuous overnight discovery

Frozen before scoring. Prior same-clock recurrence on US-24 lost gross.
US500 reclaim had only three events and agreed with own momentum on them.
The GER40 spread result concentrated in one week. This test uses a different
information boundary: fresh cash-session repricing after the premarket price.
A cash-opening move against its overnight peer-relative move may reflect
temporary liquidity demand, or permanent information arriving at the open.
Evidence on opening price pressure in US equities motivates the distinction
(https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022109011000684); it does not establish CFD edge.
The shares diversify the research universe and have low indicative margin,
while their fees, short access and point-in-time EUR conversion remain unknown.
Another index spread threshold or narrower reclaim window would revisit
exposed evidence without adding an information source.

Use the original 17 US-24 symbols, all complete weeks from 2025-09-22
through 2026-09-14 exclusive, and weekdays in New York time. Prior scheduled
weekday 16:00, current 09:25 and 09:45 closes must exist. No backward scan
for a missing previous close. Define overnight and opening returns in bps.
Subtract the respective equal-weight cross-sectional means using the same
eligible names, at least eight. Decision at 09:45, entry at the 09:50 close,
and exit at 10:35, with all ten closes required. One long candidate per day.
Do not use future path availability to choose or replace a candidate.

Compare a fixed rank favoring opening reversal against overnight disagreement
(negative opening relative return only for names whose overnight relative
return was positive; require negative opening relative return) with a
matched unrestricted opening reversal rank at the same decision clocks.
Also compare the expected uniform one-name long on the same predecision
candidate set and cash. Alphabetic ties. One candidate in each ranked policy
per selected day. Use paired available outcomes for the two ranked policies;
uniform is reported on complete eligible-cohort days with coverage separate.
Report every availability stage, model available-case, both chronological
segments (first 25 and last 26 calendar weeks), gross bps, sampled excursion
and weekly block uncertainty. Use no retuning, secondary variants or
fees from other symbols. Follow-up cost recovery requires positive gross
and positive paired increment versus opening reversal in both segments.
The archived BID bars give gross proxies. EUR NET, funded paths and
independent confirmation remain unknown. All dates are exposed development.
