## Economic source expansion

The first aligned extension spans 13 complete weeks from 27 Jul to 26 Oct 2026, with native bar warmup from 13 Jul. This starts when the shorter CVP1 scope ends and stops at the start of the original full-session archive. The chosen window covers market transitions before the three previously studied weeks, without selecting dates on returns. The collector uses the six full-session instruments and adds AUDUSD for a different major-FX session profile and NAS100 for an equity-index relative-motion comparison. The named symbols are resolved against the original broker roster. Price acquisition uses observable native activity. Disabled status, trading mode, volume, expected margin and quote currency are recorded separately for assessment of funded policies. These eight requested symbols define the registered source job. The C# historical runner accepts the documented eligible symbols in each authenticated source.

The job uses the existing view-only Pepperstone cTrader Open API authenticator. It binds the original account and symbol identity, captures M1/M5/M15/M30/H1, and requests both tick sides over hours evidenced by any of those native bars. EURUSD supplies the executable-side conversion when observed. The archive preserves provider availability, original contract fields, schedule timezone and the margin snapshot timestamp. Current restrictions or missing margin evidence only affect financial admission.

Each truncated tick response remains sealed with its request and exact descendant pages. Readback independently checks the full preorder tree against the roots derived from the native bars, for BID and ASK separately. Terminal intervals alone count toward verified requested coverage. NO_QUOTES and empty terminal pages are reported separately. Missing or unexpected pages, broken partitions, unsupported provider errors and unresolved same-millisecond truncations fail validation. Raw responses already received, including the failed shard, are retained with the failure coordinates. [Spotware tick pagination](https://help.ctrader.com/open-api/symbol-data/#attain-historical-tick-data)

The 91-day ceiling is 17,472 original tick requests across both sides, 2,440 native-bar pages and 19 identity or contract requests. Splitting every original tick root once requires at most 54,875 requests. The job budgets 64,000, leaving 9,125 for further subdivision in that stress case. The preceding authenticated full-session capture used 5,581 requests in 1,534 seconds. At the same average transport and custody rate, the full allowance takes about 293 minutes. A 315-minute collector deadline allows a further 15 minutes before the 330-minute job limit to preserve and upload original shards. The request schedule takes at least 250 ms between historical request starts. Spotware does not guarantee historical availability or tick density for the new cohort. [Reference capture](https://github.com/mariancradulescu/mxm-quant-cloud/actions/runs/44687919474) · [Open API limits](https://help.ctrader.com/open-api/) · [Actions limits](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/limits)

The independent C# coverage job reports matched contexts, full quote paths and potential decision clocks by ISO week and instrument. The reported ENTER/WAIT and CLOSE/KEEP source clocks require a ready observation and two subsequent complete price marks. These counts measure the available observations for an entry or management comparison. They establish neither a funded state nor a settled label. A separate authorized economic run must produce those labels and the financed comparisons.

Later extensions should prioritize absent joint observations where they add novel economics. EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD and AUD quoted instruments need original conversion routes and appropriate contract and margin evidence rather than enforced USD assumptions. H4/D1 can supply slow state once the broker's actual sessions, DST and bar publication are available for the hypothesis. Higher resolution is useful where it resolves real protective or management path ambiguity. Capture-date contract information alone cannot reconstruct historical funding; a dated authentic record or explicitly conditional treatment is required. Major holidays, FX rollover, early equity closes and simultaneous intermarket availability should be measured from the actual calendars and observations. ## Chronological experiment

One FIRST label pilot starts at EUR300 for each friction. It advances at every observation including gaps between label windows. Each label splits its exact current cash, lifecycle, pending orders, held margin, risk state and positions into two copies. Compare ENTER with WAIT and CLOSE with KEEP under the same subsequent FIRST lifecycle. Other funded opportunities can displace either branch. Both trajectories must settle at an observable common flat clock. Unknown paths, unverified carry and right-censorship never supply zero labels. In the historical mode the two branches may mature into a later week before the common source boundary. The pilot and the evaluated portfolio carry exposures across UTC days and week boundaries.

Folds use full calendar weeks. Reserve the first complete week for fit and the next for calibration. At each following Monday use all labels matured strictly before the previous Monday for expanding fit. Recalibrate on decisions from the intervening week that settled before the new Monday. Later-settling rows cannot inform that snapshot. Score test rows with the snapshot from their own decision week. Start every comparable evaluation policy at EUR300 once and preserve the portfolio and order state across each weekly model refresh. The preset four-week prospective calendar and its embargoes are separate. The model coefficients, risk budget, lifecycle barriers, costs and outcome definitions receive no hindsight tuning.

The economic hypotheses are that witnessed information about continuation and failed auctions and the current position/capital state predict funded incremental value, and that the close option adds value over entry valuation alone. Use the same independent CASH, FIRST, deterministic hash, pooled, training-week reassignment placebo, entry-only, full-context and no-exhaustion books for central and adverse. Matched Brier, mean error and coverage compare contextual guidance with pooled and placebo forecasts. Full terminal NET against CASH and FIRST under adverse friction tests economic value, and full-context versus entry-only tests the management contribution. No overlap of calibration labels with evaluation decisions, forced HARD21 entries, ex-post winner selection or annual confirmation from exposed data is allowed. Review the weekly deltas, per-instrument contribution, unknown holding paths and rollovers, sampled drawdown, peak margin and weekly funded complete closes. A censored terminal book yields no complete modeled NET. Weekly NET is comparable only between flat supported endpoints. The historical report is descriptive and does not export the entire floating mark path in its public economic report. Raw replay sources and settlement reconciliation remain available for audit under custody.

## Source custody and execution

The draft source workflow verifies on PRs. Collection and custody require the original OWNER actor on main, a first push attempt and a single commit that adds only `ops/funded-action-history-source-once.txt` once. Subject and file content are exactly `OWNER_FUNDED_ACTION_HISTORY_READ_ONLY_SOURCE_ONCE` with a final LF in the file. After authorized collection, the same workflow reopens and hashes each sealed shard and emits only the C# source coverage and its receipt. It produces no economic evaluation, orders or Cloud activation. A protected economic run requires its own authorization. [Source workflow](../.github/workflows/funded-action-history-source.yml)

For the private verified source, `Mxm.Replay --funded-action-value-historical` uses only contemporaneously available terms. When the source contains genuine paired quotes and only later original contract terms, `--funded-action-value-historical-conditional` uses that fixed snapshot across the entire historical sample as a declared conditional ledger scenario. It does not add synthetic quotes or treat those terms as historical evidence. The two modes disclose input SHA-256 and each market's observed terms date, quote/path support and accepted marks. The conditional method follows the existing OWNER statistical-cost authority and cannot certify broker fills, funding, continuous drawdown or economic qualification. [OWNER authority](../docs/OWNER_SPEC.md#owner-expansion-full-historical-research-and-statistical-costs-after-pr53)

The observation-only cBot loads the same C# snapshot format, uses the source roster from that model, and sends the platform observations to the same portfolio engine. The historical path rolls snapshots at causal boundaries. The Cloud observation adapter exposes the same Core snapshot update while ordinary startup consumes a frozen supplied snapshot. Nominally identical frames can be matched through the adapter in tests. Actual platform feed parity remains a separate read-only evidence task.
