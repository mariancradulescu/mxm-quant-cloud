Counterexamples include overlap/conflict removal, unavailable authenticated fills, cost, margin contention, risk suspensions, low utility, or sparse evaluation weeks. Hypothesis H2 (future, not yet scored): among ex-ante fundable opportunities, allocating by **risk-adjusted adverse incremental NET per committed margin-time**, against chronological first-admissible and low-margin-first controls, may improve one-book economic performance. The mechanism is joint capital allocation plus complete exit policy, not another symmetric three-bar price barrier. No parameter search is approved by this diagnostic stage.

## Five non-interchangeable quantities (computed per predeclared ISO UTC eligible week)
1. Per-family/instrument **raw** proposals, including conflicted or overlapping; never apply a family-level HARD21 admission gate.
2. All combined **raw** proposals (duplicates counted explicitly).
3. **Unique executable candidates** after deterministic exact-key coalescing, same-instrument/same-clock opposite-direction conflict rejection, then conservative same-instrument planned-holding overlap exclusion, without examining outcomes. Distinct instruments at one clock remain eligible to contend for common capital.
4. **Completed modeled financed round trips** only after unique opportunity -> one position ID mapping, immutable chronological common EUR300 Core ledger receipts, complete partial/final settlements and exact cash/NET identity. Never count partial closures, rejected proposals, independent sleeve sums or non-funded hypothetical fills.
5. **Systemic HARD21 certificate** only with all objective eligible weeks at >=21 complete financed trips *and* verified original broker contract/lot/margin, chronological BID/ASK entry/exit/full floating path, genuine fees/FX/financing, continuous DD <=30%, and independent book reconciliation. An unavailable component is UNKNOWN, not a favorable estimate.

Weekly closure attribution follows UTC ISO week of the actual final completion, not signal clock. Zero-activity eligible weeks remain. An empty or unverified eligible-week registry cannot pass. Report both raw/unique weekly counts and complete modeled trip counts; do not equate modeled with broker-real fills.
