for outcomes, and sparse current-term cost observations for conditional
sensitivity. These are two separate evidentiary levels.

  filter same-direction US500 and NAS100 H1 momentum. This tests consensus

sample with the unconditional directional pool in each stratum, weighted by

benchmark is a descriptive exposure-matched expectation, not a trading rule
or an independent sample. Cash is the absolute zero reference.
