## Economic estimand and mechanisms

The primary predicted object is the **net EUR outcome of a completed position from an executable next genuine bar open through an exit produced by its own causal lifecycle**, including entry, exit, costs and financing uncertainty. This is not a six-bar price-change forecast or a fixed-duration exit. The entry mechanism is a strictly chronological, symbol/side-conditioned empirical distribution of **completed shadow position lifecycles**, available to selection only once their exit witness has been observed. A shadow path is **unfinanced learning evidence**, never a completed financed trade, broker order or portfoliowide performance claim. Each market and direction is observed by the SAME ex-ante shadow management rule; losing, missing, and censored paths are not discarded as favorable selections.

The shadow and funded lifecycle close only following a genuinely closed native bar and submit a conditional modeled fill for the next actual bar open. Management uses a 2-ATR initial hard risk boundary, trailing 2-ATR close-confirmed structure and two consecutive contrary completed-bar closes as competing invalidation events. No invented intrabar barrier fill, global 6-bar target, normal time exit or advance information. ATR uses eight contiguous mature source-native bars. A source gap or missing valid next open is explicitly censored; it never creates a winning label. Every actual funded position additionally obeys shared-margined portfolio protection and legality of partial reductions.

All seven genuine, causally completed M1/M5/M15/M30/H1/H4/D1 contexts are eligible when available as-of; no timeframe or indicator family is declared superior. Predefined features are signed normalized two-close movement and tick-volume activity where genuinely observed, plus an unconditional completed-lifecycle prior. Online shrinkage uses a 64-sample prior; an absent feature has zero weight. Only **completed** shadow lifecycle labels (including modeled roundtrip costs) mature; no fixed-horizon future price is a target. Entry decides among LONG, SHORT and WAIT using the positive expected full-lifecycle net EUR incremental to staying in CASH, with economic size and risk restrictions. Estimates and uncertainty are reported independently and never treated as independent certification. No tuning or switching to favorable families after observing FPE1 outcomes.
