## Common full-cycle contract
The exact `NativeUnifiedPortfolioEngine.Advance(frame)` in Mxm.Core takes causally timestamped source quote and completed 5-minute and 15-minute bar observations. The SAME engine/method runs in Replay and in a new **opt-in, observation-only** pathway compiled into Mxm.Cloud. Both call the existing C# NativeFundedSession, NativeFundedLedger, NativeCausalExitResearch and SIR1 NativeSimulatedCloseLifecycle. One independent EUR300 native model book PER predeclared counterfactual policy; NO per-symbol budgets within a book, no accidental summation of policy results. Event/position/financial parity tested on identical serialized source frames, identical broker-status frames and restart checkpoints; publish complete redacted ledger event streams for original outcomes.

Broker-callback boundary is explicit: the product reads real cTrader BrokerIdentitySnapshot and platform account/position/order state, including ACCOUNT TYPE (NETTED/HEDGED/UNKNOWN), separately from simulated model intents; no real orders are submitted. A simulated REQUEST_OPEN/CLOSE is never a broker order; a real broker status must never be canceled/filled from model timeout. Treat actual account snapshots as UNKNOWN/incomplete unless original trustworthy historical scope and stable identity are available; preserve broker identity in checkpoint readbacks. If the broker offers no real historical order evidence, never claim filled orders or monetary broker NET. For real cTrader semantics use IAccount.AccountType and positions/orders/deal observations; future real order capability remains unauthorized.
