# Delayed repricing after broad altcoin moves

Research choice. The US-24 opening study lost gross after the cash boundary.
Repeating opening reversal would revisit the same information channel. Index
spread participation had a single positive week, while simultaneous index
positions consume most of EUR300. Oil futures/perpetual basis could identify
a distinct carry channel, but needs contemporaneous roll/funding, synchronous
quote and product identity evidence before interpreting convergence. This
pilot selects broad altcoin repricing across five previously untested
contract series from the verified Pepperstone development archive. Shared
liquidity and price discovery motivate breadth. Crypto-specific common
shocks can already be priced by entry, and apparent lag can be stale
quotes or token-specific news. Prior ETH weekend drift and crypto-to-FX
tests do not estimate this same-market cross-sectional channel.

Frozen design. Use XLMUSD and POLUSD as targets (established assets from
distinct token ecosystems) and all four remaining names as peers in each
case, from ATMUSD, GLMRUSD, POLUSD, TRUMPUSD, XLMUSD. Use all 51 full weeks
from 2025-09-22 to 2026-09-14 exclusive, all days at 06, 10, 14, 18 UTC.
Require 13 exact M5 BID closes through decision for every name. Calculate
log bps over the completed hour. Require at least three peers of four to
agree with the sign of the peer median, absolute peer median at least
25 bps, and the absolute target return no more than half that median
magnitude. Predict continuation in the peer direction after a five-minute
entry delay and hold 60 minutes. Require every target BID close during
holding. These are price proxies and do not attest fill or quote freshness.
Decide before inspecting the path. Count both missing decision and path
coverage, and include weekends without treating them as independent.

Compare to own last-hour momentum, matched long, matched short and cash
at identical signal clocks. Flat own momentum abstains. Also report the
number of opposite/flat/same own-momentum directions on priced paths.
Report first 25 and last 26 weeks, all weeks and descriptive four-week
block intervals. No pooling of returns across targets, no optimized
threshold, no selection of a more favorable symbol/window. To advance a
target to cost verification require positive absolute and own-momentum
incremental gross in both segments and positive full-period incremental
gross versus the stronger constant side. This is a screen for price
information, not a trading gate or proof of EUR loss from signed bps.
New costs are not prohibited by failure of this exact mechanism.

Report original hash-bound data and indicative minimum-margin probes.
Own-symbol bid/ask, physical volume, applicable funding, commission,
EUR conversion and historical eligibility are required to price NET.
A positive screen warrants targeted authenticated read-only recovery and
existing common C# replay if evidence supports it. The entire source is
exposed development. No orders, Cloud, independent or funded claims.
