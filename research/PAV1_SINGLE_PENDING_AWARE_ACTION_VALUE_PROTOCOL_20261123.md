## Primary research question
Does **causal portfolio-level counterfactual ACTION VALUE** relative to an actual common EUR300 CASH/WAIT policy, learned from the joint economic reward / risk / capital occupancy of sequential decisions, justify opening, holding, partial reduction or closing a position? Not another six-bar forecast or a full-position shadow win-frequency selector. Policy is directly evaluated as a **multi-action, continuously financed decision problem**, not isolated signals.

The distinct causal mechanism is **online discounted economic Bellman action value with multiple competing actions**, using strictly *matured observed market transitions*, execution-friction estimates, funding, locked margin, cross-market opportunity cost, downside risk and visible-account equity. Discount 0.96 and learning shrinkage prior 64 are ex-ante engineering assumptions, not optimized outcomes. Observations include genuine completed M1/M5/M15/M30/H1/H4/D1 contexts where as-of available, observed tick activity only when genuine, clock gaps and time-since-last-liquid mark; no family/timeframe deemed superior. Missing context is flagged, never synthesized; higher-timeframe candles are not anticipated from incompletely closed bars.

For each symbol/side, learning updates expected **per-event realizable incremental EUR reward and downside tail**, using only preceding complete observed state transition and known closing mark at the subsequent genuine bar. These are learning evidence, NOT funded trips. At a funded clock, estimate action-specific LONG/SHORT (pay entry and eventual expected close friction + margin occupancy), WAIT (0), HOLD (preserve option versus close), REDUCE (legally sized margin release versus incremental hold) and CLOSE (reclaim margin and stop downside). Optimize prospective incremental cash-equivalent net EUR conditional to state, including opportunity cost of reserved capital. Trading cost is applied once per action in common ledger; expected future cost is a reserve, not certified historical execution. No fixed price prediction horizon is the signal/exit timer. Holding/exit decisions are recomputed from new fully closed observations plus portfolio-wide capacity, with explicit protection in loss.
