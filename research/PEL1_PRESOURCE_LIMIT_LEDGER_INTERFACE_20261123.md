It must preserve cash, all cost components, event hashes, position occupancy and original-side executable quote records; must reject favorable fill without available quote history or accept at no price better than the original frozen limit. This is an additive event vocabulary necessary to honor the frozen economic thesis, **not a new ledger or a revaluation of old results**.

All other ex-ante protocol parameters, guard budgets, cost stress, universe, population, comparison books, and economic falsifiers remain fixed. An implementation-specific need for a changed threshold or new mechanism after reading any PEL1 outcome would instead invalidate the original evaluation and cannot be treated as a PEL1 repair or retune.
