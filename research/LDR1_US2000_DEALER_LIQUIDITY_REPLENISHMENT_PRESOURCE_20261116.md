## Narrow causal economic mechanism and distinction

US2000 CFD dealer/hedging prices reflect US small-cap index risk and OTC liquidity provision. When broker-displayed top-of-book liquidity on one side is depleted then **restored at an unchanged two-sided spot quote**, such replenishment could indicate temporary demand absorption by liquidity providers, rather than a price trend. Predicted future 60–180s quote movement would be toward the replenished side (BID restored => long bias; ASK restored => short bias). Competing explanations include synthetic broker DOM, resized dealer quotes not executable, immediate efficient information incorporation, adverse market news, and spread/slippage overwhelming any effect. This is *hypothesis*, not proven. The channel is observed broker-displayed quote size, not trade prints, exchange market depth or identity of institutional flow.
