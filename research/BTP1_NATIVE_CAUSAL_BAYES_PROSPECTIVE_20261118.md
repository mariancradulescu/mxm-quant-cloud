The null is no improvement against CASH and the same-clock funded simple persistence/contrarian controls. An independent chronological, market-specific source with complete quotes and contract terms is necessary to test this hypothesis.

The implementation is in **`src/Mxm.Core/NativeBayesianCommonEpisode.cs`** (single prospective authoritative model, decisions, observed-quote exit requests and `NativeFundedLedger`). Frozen settings: Beta(2,2) symmetric prior for upward transitions, count only nonzero sequential completed observed midpoint changes, posterior thresholds long >=0.70, short <=0.30, at least 4 matured transitions. Observe closed marks only; no retrospective labels. One synthetic physical unit; latency 1000 ms, simulated fill **at the NEXT explicitly supplied observed BID/ASK** (never at the forecast clock), quote slippage 0.005. SL is 2x observed mean absolute transition from signal quote (minimum width 0.01); TP is 2x that stop distance. Risk exit triggers only at explicitly observed quote sides and fills only at the next valid observed quote. Maximum duration 30 minutes is safety only, not the primary target. Costs, conversions, financing, equity, margin and closed NET use the existing C# `NativeFundedLedger` event machine; missing proof must not become zero cost. No genuine broker stop order is simulated.
