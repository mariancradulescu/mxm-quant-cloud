overlap transmission hypothesis there needs synchronous original priced paths
and GER40 contractual evidence. The conserved MSP1 reference summaries alone
cannot supply those paths. XAUUSD has larger reference movement and consumes

precedent in PR #262–263. The expanded US-24 universe retains an open physical

Use the already sealed NXE1 source for a test that needs one tradable market.
Test whether a completed failed excursion from the US cash open, followed by
a reclaim of the opening level, identifies direction and sufficient subsequent
EUR amplitude. The prior cash-session US500 volatility-persistence rule used
range and directional agreement. This rule uses an excursion, recovery and
opening anchor within the same session. It does not trade the session overlap
as such, rerun a prior rule, or use US30 confirmation.

## Fixed design

US500 only on all 17 exposed original NXE1 weeks from 2026-07-13, weekdays
11:00, 12:30 and 14:00 America/New_York. Require all five-minute model-ready
marks from 09:30 through decision, using the opening mid as the anchor.
At decision, a long requires the midpoint to have spent at least one
observed clock 20 bps below the anchor and the previous midpoint to be
strictly below the anchor with the latest midpoint at or above. A short is
the mirror. If both historical excursions occurred, the latest crossing
still determines the side. An opening reclaim can represent exhaustion of
transient inventory pressure, or ordinary intraday momentum through a
reference level. The latter is tested with a 30-minute own-midpoint momentum
control at the same selected clocks. Retain matched long, short and cash
controls. Only one candidate instrument is used in this comparison.

Enter at the next original five-minute mark and hold 60 minutes. Keep decision
eligibility, selected intent and subsequently unpriced paths separate.
Require every holding mark and signed EUR conversion, same original
authenticated US500 0.1 unit terms with zero commission and conversion fee,
and known rollover outside the interval. Use the existing US500 protected
source verification and markout cost function: observed bid/ask, modeled
slippage per side and an additional declared adverse spread/slippage scenario.
Terms and margin are observed later and price a historical sensitivity only.
Report EUR nominal, sampled excursions, central/adverse outcomes, weekly
density and both fixed calendar segments (first eight, last nine). The
original C# prospective as-of boundary remains applicable to admission.

Proceed to an as-of funded study only if matched adverse EUR is positive in
both segments and the combined result exceeds cash, matched long/short and
own-momentum. Sparse signals and unknown outcomes must be visible. No target
retuning, replacement source windows, broker orders or Cloud activation.
