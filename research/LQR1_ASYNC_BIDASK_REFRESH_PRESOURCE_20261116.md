## Economic premise, specified before new scoring

When a broker independently updates BID and ASK, the **signed direction of the last individually changed quote-side price** may contain temporary dealer price-discovery information different from the sum of upward/downward revisions, their temporal concentration, and a completed OHLC midpoint momentum. The opposite quote-side may be stale and subsequently follow the most recently revised side. Alternatively, quote refreshing is transient spread management with no executable-price continuation. The prediction is falsified economically if the incremental conditional after-cost EUR return does not exceed both cash and simple same-clock own-midpoint momentum, or if independent quote-side timing is unobservable.

The observation is strictly past original individual-side BID and ASK tick timestamps and prices. It is **not executed trade direction, order flow, Level II, volume, latent customer inventory or assumed latency arbitrage**. No historical P&L subgroup was used to select the rule.
