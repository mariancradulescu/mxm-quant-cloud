## 1. What economic service could be rewarded?
An electronic CFD quote oscillates at short horizons. Unlike crossing the market spread immediately, a non-marketable **buy limit at the currently observed BID** (sell limit at ASK) seeks a favorable price and gives up fill certainty; if subsequently price reverts to a sufficiently distant executable level, liquidity provision by waiting can earn price improvement. This is NOT a broker rebate, proof of exchange market-making, possession of level-2 queue priority, or a guarantee that a displayed quote is tradeable; Pepperstone CFD prices are broker quotes. Adverse selection and missed fills are the competing explanations. If improvement vanishes after missed fills, first-quote latency, costs, stop slippage or inventory drawdown, the economic claim is false.

**Distinct falsifiable question:** does this time-price execution *mechanism* produce positive total conservative EUR NET and improve whole-book EUR NET versus the **same direction, same offered time, same portfolio financing but immediately crossed executable market entry** when combined with identical causal inventory/exit rules? A separate fixed-fill-time counterfactual strips the alleged price improvement. This does not use a new fitted return predictor or re-optimize a frozen signal. It is intentionally a service/transaction economics hypothesis, not a guarantee of directional alpha.

## 3. Precise observable decision and limit fill rules (unchangeable after outcomes)
- Only at **completed genuine M5** bar clocks within original 08:00–10:45 UTC Monday-Friday session and after warmup 20 completed contiguous native M5 bars. No current incomplete bar or future tick in an offer. For each market at most one held/pending exposure, one potential offer per M5. M1/M15/M30/H1/H4/D1 are available for audit/gap and risk context, but **not compulsory features**: they cannot become an after-results filter. The intended side uses no fitted model: if last completed M5 close is **below** the arithmetic midpoint of that same bar (high+low)/2, intend LONG; if above, intend SHORT; exact equality -> WAIT. This fixed sign is deliberately a weak *non-directional-repricing control*; matched taker and fixed-time comparisons share it. We make no predictive claim from this bar geometry.
- Decision at first genuine fresh, noncrossed BID/ASK quote **strictly after completed bar close**, <=30-second freshness and EURUSD FX quote. Proposal passive limit at BID for long or ASK for short. Fixed validity until next M5 decision, never extended after outcomes. Simulated order remains pending and **reserves its full legal minimum margin and stop-risk throughout**. No pseudo-fill by candle high/low. A subsequent quote-side condition must persist at **two genuine distinct after-intent ticks spaced >=100 ms**: BUY executable ASK is <= buy limit minus 1 authenticated tick size; SELL executable BID >= sell limit + 1 tick. Both side quotes must be noncrossed and each <=30 sec old. Fill model takes **exact original limit price, not favorable improvement**; then call the same native-funded OPEN_FILL and full post-fill cost/margin guard. No actual broker execution or queue-position claim. Unfilled or ambiguous intents cancel only at expiration, after a real timestamp, release reserve then; unknown fill/cancel ordering is never called filled.
- Target / normal management: entry-frozen executable liquidation price sufficient to cover one full modeled round-trip commission/spread/slippage cost plus **one current tick** of genuinely favorable additional movement. Request CLOSE only on an observed future fresh quote *and* positive estimated net liquidation, or on price/structural invalidation. Entry-frozen protective stop is one entire **last completed M5 true range** adverse of limit entry (plus conservative roundtrip friction in reserved risk); risk monitoring on every observed quote, never on assumed OHLC barrier fill. A genuinely newly completed M5 price invalidation is also a close reason. The next fresh quote beyond trigger settles at actual modeled executable BID/ASK with full adverse extra slippage, not the barrier price. HOLD otherwise; REDUCE only if original volume grid permits a strictly smaller still-legal remainder (at minimum it is explicitly infeasible and must not be forged); ALLOCATE is only common-capital financed decision. Position at source boundary remains OPEN/uncertain if real quote cannot support close; no favorable terminal liquidation. **No ordinary duration-only exit.** Session-end is a separately flagged protective failsafe request, not a profit-target mechanism.
- Immutable same-clock separate EUR300 full-book comparators: PASSIVE (primary), TAKER (identical side/offers, crosses current quote with modeled latency, identical position management), and FILL_TIME_TAKER (when the passive order did fill, cross same clock as passive fill at genuine quote; tracks fill selection, not independent fundable alpha); CASH. LONG-only/SHORT-only descriptive cohorts are not pooled into the primary. **No after-outcome benchmark winner shopping.**
- Model stress: current cost terms x1, x1.5, x2 on spread/slippage, fee/FX and financing separately with nonzero conservative reserve; model conditional intrabar risk as unknown wherever ticks are absent. No invented maker rebates. The scenario x1.5 is the preregistered primary adverse gate; x2 must be disclosed without choosing a lower stress ex post. Do not claim historical executable quotation/fills certified.
