## Exactly one hypothesis, predeclared before reading BTI1 outcomes

For an otherwise identical observable ten-minute own-midpoint momentum direction, *clustering of the timestamps of independent BID/ASK price revisions*, conditional on activity count, can identify short-lived dealer repricing and elevated adverse quote-execution risk. Abstaining during concentrated bursts might preserve capital relative to unconditional momentum and an activity-count-only gate. Burstiness is NOT trade flow, DOM, executed volume, directional alpha, an order-fill model, or a name for QPI1's existing signed revision counts. Failure is expected to be reportable without tuning.

Mathematical distinction: holding each side's ordered price-revision values and the total up/down counts fixed, relocating the update timestamps within a ten-minute window changes BTI1's minute-bin collision probability but leaves QPI1's U,D,(U-D)/(U+D), and the ten-minute own endpoint momentum unchanged. Thus BTI1 contains potentially new *timestamp placement* information; whether this is useful beyond count, spread and momentum is an empirical null test, not assumed.

## Presignal information and strictly fixed feature

At decision t require paired BID/ASK as-of at t-10m and t, each no more than 20 seconds old, positive and never crossed. Own-momentum side = sign(mid_t - mid_(t-10m)); side 0 is CASH. Within [t-600000ms,t], for EACH side count strictly changing quote prices vs the last prior observed price of that same side, including the pre-window baseline when present. Ignore unchanged values. Every revision gets its original event millisecond timestamp. N=total number of bid and ask strictly changing revisions. QPI-style net U,D counts are logged as a control only; QPI policy is NEVER scored again.

Use 10 fixed consecutive 60-second bins, starting at t-600000ms (an event at t belongs to the final bin). n_j is total price-revision events in bin j. For N>=12, burst concentration C=sum_j n_j(n_j-1)/(N(N-1)); C>=0.20 means BURST and C<0.20 means QUIET. For N<12, state is INSUFFICIENT_ACTIVITY and both BTI1 and count-only ABSTAIN. No forward-price or future event is used in feature construction. This statistic is event-time distribution, not a transformation of OHLC bars or net price direction. A count-only ablation cannot use event placement.

Decision policy: QUIET and nonzero momentum -> follow momentum; BURST or insufficient activity -> CASH. At other identical clocks the pure MOMENTUM control follows the same own-momentum side regardless of C (N>=12), and CASH remains neutral. COUNT-ONLY abstains when N>= the median N of the first four weeks of *eligible predecision source clocks on the same target*, otherwise follows the same momentum side. The count median is determined without any future return label and locked for weeks 5–8; missing median -> abstain/unknown, never infer a value. Matched constant-long and constant-short fixed-side economics are additional disclosures, not alternative selection rules.
