## 1. Economic diagnosis before implementation

The corrected SDP2 original `44038569445` / `14308483122` yielded 104 frozen weekly models; FULL's adverse target MSE was 2.130180 (60m) and 2.636375 (240m), both worse than the prespecified zero-price-movement plus known modeled costs comparator (2.086487 / 2.622372). FULL and NO_PEERS were executable-prediction identical. Every fitted FULL adverse LONG/SHORT output was bounded above by a negative value, hence no positive adverse action could ever reach the existing native selector. The result is *not* evidence that all prices are unpredictable; it is direct evidence that this fitted function does not offer an economically admissible action.

A return label can be decomposed descriptively as `directional price change in EUR − entry/exit spread − commission − FX conversion − slippage assumption − financing`, with timing, legal volume and currency routes preserved. Cost predictability can reduce action-NET MSE without learning future price changes. Directionally positive realized labels (about 34–42% in SDP2, depending on side/horizon) are counterfactual prevalence, not an as-of positive-value policy. Also, preserving EUR300 in CASH is equality to CASH, never investment performance. The exact SDP2 dataset and forecasts are historical diagnostics only; their thresholds and output functions are not altered or rerun.

**New falsifiable question:** Does any additional, as-of cBot-observable information change *which feasible action to take* and improve the marginal common-book EUR outcome against a matched lower-information policy after authentic or explicitly conditional costs, rather than merely reducing unconditional error? The object of research is the incremental **decision**, not the number of models or the count of favorable future price moves.

## 2. State/action and estimand, before any forecast

There are TWO different decision states, which must not be mixed in one binary "predict UP" target:

* **Flat with no pending symbol exposure**: feasible actions are OPEN_LONG, OPEN_SHORT and CASH, subject to current legal volume/step, free margin, account drawdown headroom and evidence gates. CASH has zero trading payoff and is always a possible reference.
* **Existing long/short, including pending risk**: actions KEEP and EXIT refer to the same actual or explicitly hypothetical position, its opening basis, accrued finance, current spread, FX conversion, current liquidation value and shared portfolio exposures. EXIT consumes the actual closing cost at t; KEEP carries future spread/gap/finance risk and deferred exit costs. An infeasible new action is NOT assigned a favorable return.

For each action and state, define a prospective causal **incremental EUR value** relative to an action available to the *same book* at the *same decision time*, with exactly the same prior capital and exposures: `DeltaV_t(a,b) = conditional NAV_(next decision after a) − conditional NAV_(next decision after b)`. Both paths must follow the same declared subsequent decision and liquidation rule; future prices, available future quotes, and position survivorship cannot affect the action offered at t. If an action cannot be uniquely evaluated from genuine source quotes/marks/costs, mark its outcome UNKNOWN; never manufacture a zero or favorable fill. Terminal net, cumulative financing, paired counterfactual paths and sampled floating equity remain distinct measurements.

The final exact experiment must fix (before any newly scored outcomes): its time grid, horizon/next-decision transition, shadow state construction if applicable, source/contract versions, quote staleness, withdrawal/suspension mechanics, legal volume, currency conversion, fees and gap assumptions. In particular, hypothetical entry states are NOT completed financed trades. A HOLD-vs-EXIT oracle using future peaks is forbidden as a policy input. If historical held positions are unavailable, the policy can still study **explicitly labeled hypothetical state-value contrasts**; it cannot claim demonstrated exit-policy NET.

## 4. Predictive estimands and mandatory comparators for a LATER exact scoring protocol

The first scoring programme, if the census demonstrates enough comparable action paths, must pre-register one causal decision-value method and fixed ablations BEFORE labels are inspected. Its training outputs are expected *marginal action value* and adverse monetary loss/uncertainty for each feasible action; mere directional sign confidence, unweighted price MSE and after-the-fact best-market selection are not sufficient. No algorithm, horizon, timestamp or hyperparameter is selected by SDP2's exposed good/bad weeks. New labels mature at the **last evaluated exposure/exit** plus a declared embargo; an earlier clock may only use labels already matured at that clock. Calendar overlap with prior studies is declared exposed irrespective of different local model folds.

Predeclare a matched set of policies evaluated on **identical clocks and initial EUR300 capital**, at minimum:
- CASH (flat) / immediate protective EXIT (held) as conservative references; HOLD must be evaluated against the same-position EXIT, not a different randomly opened position;
- no-new-information decision using only legally observable state, quotes, current costs and financing;
- a simple own-price/position-state benchmark with identical eligibility and capital constraints;
- the **incremental-information** policy and a frozen ablation of the one new information channel; cost-only (zero expected price move) and information-permutation/null diagnostics, without a retrospective winner.

Each is a separate counterfactual path on one EUR300 founding account, not portfolios whose profits can be summed. Mandatory score order is: (a) completeness/censoring by every scheduled clock; (b) paired causal action/value and uncertainty, distinguishing price contribution from friction/cost contribution; (c) incremental common-book NET EUR central and adverse after fees, conversion and financing, if genuine matched valuations exist; (d) complete **financed** weekly closures toward systemic HARD21 and sampled peak-to-trough floating DD including open/pending positions and gap-unknown; (e) truly subsequent independent chronological confirmation, never an exposed 2025–26 partition relabeled blind.

No success on a proxy may be called executable NET. A higher-return policy that violates available margin, has missing adverse marks, or exceeds modeled 30% peak-to-floating-equity DD cannot be promoted. If all estimated feasible action values are nonpositive, abstain and report failure *without* forced trades; the research may still have identified a useful absence of information.
