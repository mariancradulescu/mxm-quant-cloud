## 1 — Economic premise / new information
Rather than minimizing average forecast MSE or repeating a fixed BTC-leading direction, learn *which of three executable ETHUSD actions* (LONG, SHORT, CASH) is advantageous in minimum-volume EUR after spread, fee, conversion and slippage using matured historically **paired** actions. Test whether adding contemporaneously completed BTCUSD return sign to ETHUSD sign changes the actual causal action chosen and improves matched conditional common-book NET compared with the same value learner with ETH-only state or no state. Different conditional-information *value-learning* method from the frozen CXL1 BTC-leads trading rule and the frozen SDP2 pooled boosted regression; no optimized variants or retuning of either.
Competing nulls: spread/commission absorb price moves; BTC sign adds no marginal information; finite week dependence falsely inflates action-value estimates; naive own-price sign does as well as or better than any learned value; nonstationary joint distributions make an earlier conditional average misleading. CASH is 0 payoff, not alpha; errors that are reduced by predictable costs do not establish price information.

## 4 — Fixed causal policy, no searched hyperparameters
At each evaluation T estimate conditional *adverse EUR* mean for each side separately from all previously matured complete action labels. Three fixed information sets:
- `POOLED`: all matured past labels; no return direction state.
- `OWN`: labels from the same ETH sign E, excluding BTC context.
- `FULL`: labels from the same (ETH sign E, BTC sign B) pair; **only** additional information is BTC sign.
Each set uses shrinkage toward CASH zero with exactly **32 virtual observations** (`sum(adverse label)/(n+32)`). Minimum observed support `n>=20` AND `>=4` distinct calendar weeks before selection. Uncertainty: one standard error of **week-block means**, computed across those complete calendar weeks with eligible state observations; no row-independent intervals. Score side = shrunk mean minus exactly `1.0 * SE_of_weekly_block_means`. Choose higher positive score; if both nonpositive, insufficient or tied, CASH (a tie of strictly positive scores also CASH). No direction inversion, threshold search, alternative windows, reconditioning on current exit availability or retrospectively selected asset/time. Update only with matured previous outcomes after fixed embargo. No modeled position at T if quote/contract/risk constraints unknown.
Report for each policy every clock's as-of forecast/action (including CASH/UNKNOWN), paired realized potential LONG/SHORT adverse and central EUR labels where complete, selected counterfactual payoff, chosen-action gross price move and friction separately, cost-only zero-move comparator and weekly 4+4 calibration of positive-outcome probability. MAIN outcome is incremental actual capital-funded native `FULL - OWN` and `FULL - POOLED` and `FULL - CASH` adverse central/NET EUR over **same 200 evaluation clocks**. FULL cannot pass on a merely less-negative-than-OWN result if FULL versus CASH is nonpositive. No selected winning variant after outcome. Additional all-LONG/all-SHORT potential payoffs are *unfunded labels only*; do not rerun CXL1 closed comparator books.
