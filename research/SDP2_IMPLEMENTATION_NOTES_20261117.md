# SDP2 pre-source implementation details

Protocol c7891655084de2f87dbf9af41f841cf6f1d0a41a remains unchanged. No authentic SDP2 outcomes have been opened at this implementation stage.

- sklearn's deterministic squared-error depth-2 trees operate on train-only quartile bins. Leaf residuals are shrunk by 32 weighted pseudo-observations, 16 rounds at 0.1. The four action-payoff targets are fitted jointly, not four separately searched models. Exported arrays use a C# arithmetic evaluator and are checked at each weekly fit.
- Training ends before calibration starts, with an additional four-hour outcome embargo; calibration itself ends at least four hours before forecast week. Calibration is a past residual diagnostic of the current fit, not a claim of archived live forecasts from four weeks earlier.
- Historical M5 bid-like closes anchor conditional model bids. Mean observed training spread (central) or p90 (adverse) supplies a modeled ask increment. The remaining declared roundtrip friction, including assumed slippage, is charged equally through the existing ledger's fee fields. Those fields therefore represent combined **modeled residual frictions**, not certified broker commission breakdowns. Costs never enter the feature vector.
- Original min/step volumes and current source margin per legal unit remain physical quantities. Contemporaneous completed M5 EURUSD and other conversion routes supply model reference EUR rates; source-observed maximum credit/debit conversion wedge is applied symmetrically around that reference (credit minus half the wedge, debit plus half). This is a current-source conditional envelope, not historically certified FX spreads. All these are explicit conditional scenarios.
- Financing is charged at each observed crossing of the original current-contract rollover clock, threefold directional debit, using that mark's notional. Funding unknown prevents new crossing exposure; an unresolved held exposure remains in the book. Missing quotes cannot cancel exposure or reset capital.
- Request entry at completed decision clock +1ms; model fill at next available M5 mark, cancelled if more than 5 minutes late. Request close one M5 step before the registered holding maturity; a missing quote can delay the actual close and is disclosed. No future label/path availability enters admissions.
- Residual quantile states across candidate assets are comonotonic artificial scenarios, NOT claimed historical joint states. Existing open/pending exposure reserves full non-offsetting stress in every state. Forecast error dispersion is not a certified future loss bound. Effective blocks=4 remains deliberately conservative for the four calibration weeks.
- Weekly labels/marginal hypothetical action returns are never a capital-funded portfolio. Only the native book reports funded modeled roundtrips and its event-chain hash. Full closed NET is reported together with open/pending and missing-mark flags; incomplete books cannot be presented as complete terminal wealth.
- All 26 evaluation weeks count for HARD21, including weeks with unavailable features or no entries. Normal-approximation weekly intervals are descriptive; overlapping horizons, serial dependence and global source exposure bar independent confirmation or promotion.

The first panel integrates all 12 original annual symbols, not all archives in the repository. Other archives, live-only DOM and broader instruments have **not** been assimilated by this bounded execution. No assertion of exhaustive information-channel discovery follows.
