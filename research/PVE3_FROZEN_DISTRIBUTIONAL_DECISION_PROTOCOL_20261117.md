## Exact features, predictive map and model (not a searched indicator grid)
At decision T, compute completed strict M15, H1 and H4 bars using only intervals ending <=T:

Also complete H1 4-hour log return, H1 tick-volume anomaly last/mean(previous 7) −1, H1 volatility acceleration RMS(last 4 adjacent) / RMS(previous 8 adjacent) −1, H1 directional efficiency |sum 8 returns| / sum |8 returns|, H4 compression last true range / median(previous 7 ranges) −1 and H4 4-bar slope normalized by its own vol.
