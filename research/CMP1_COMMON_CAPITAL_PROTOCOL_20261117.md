## Fixed new mechanisms and causal prediction (not fitted on future outcomes)

For each scheduled source event at `t`, evaluate all symbols observable at `t`. Use *only* prior observations with `Exit < t` and valid `OutcomeBps`:
- **Own past-price signal:** arithmetic mean of the latest four matured raw 120-minute own returns, clipped to ±150 bps.
- **Peer information:** for each *other* instrument with at least four matured returns, take its latest matured return less than 72 hours old, divide by its mean absolute value of its last four matured returns (floor 10 bps); equally average all such peer standardized returns; multiply by the target symbol's mean absolute return from its last four matured observations (floor 10 bps), and clip to ±150 bps. Require at least two peers, no peer weight or sign chosen by its observed profitability.
- **Joint prediction:** arithmetic mean of own and peer signal. `OWN` ablation uses only own; `PEER` uses only peer; `JOINT` uses both. Direction from signed prediction, no reversal or hindsight retuning.
- **Confidence/abstention:** from own last four matured returns calculate sample standard deviation / sqrt(4). A candidate enters only when absolute predicted return exceeds `2 * source CostBps + 0.5 * standard error`; no retrospective threshold optimization. Nonpositive signal, missing warmup or incomplete source yields abstention.
- **Ranking:** for simultaneously available opportunities, prioritize descending `(|predicted bps| - 2*CostBps - 0.5*SE) / max(10, own average absolute matured bps)`, stable symbol tie-break. Identical score inputs for `JOINT_UNRANKED` but sort alphabetically. No fixed number of instruments; no summing independently funded strategies.
