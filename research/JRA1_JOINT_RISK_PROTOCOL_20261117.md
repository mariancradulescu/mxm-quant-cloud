Combine with *full remaining* stressed monetary loss budget for every **held/pending** position (do not discount correlation or offset gains), including current liquidation costs. Reject when current native sampled equity or held marks unknown, contract/FX/financing costs unknown, observed peak/cumulative stressed loss implies drawdown >=30%, model cash <=0, funding insufficient, or any minimum exposure exceeds remaining pretrade headroom `currentMarkedEquity - 0.70 * max(300, sampledPeak)`. This historical 3x adverse scenario is **NOT a hard tail or gap bound**; it neither proves DD <=30% nor that a stop will fill. NEVER substitute margin for economic loss.

Realize model fills exclusively at actual original quote clocks using the **existing `NativeFundedSession` / `NativeFundedLedger`** via `DecideResearch`, `ApplyResearchFacts`, and native `OPEN_FILL`, `FINANCING`, `CLOSE_FILL`; use the **existing** `NativeProductAdverseBook` as the stricter repricing scenario on exactly the same facts.
